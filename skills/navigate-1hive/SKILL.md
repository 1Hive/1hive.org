# Navigate 1Hive

## When to use

Use when someone is new to 1Hive, wants help finding a contribution, or needs a map of the community's tools. Read [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) first.

## Inputs

- The human's interests and skills, and available time if relevant.
- Their question or goal. If missing, offer a short orientation before asking one focused question.
- Public sources you can access. Access to private spaces is not required.

## Workflow

1. Read the [mission and values](https://1hive.org/Our-mission-and-values), [Honey overview](https://1hive.org/Why-Honey), and [ways to contribute](https://1hive.org/How-you-can-contribute).
2. Use the [infrastructure map](https://1hive.org/Navigating-core-community-infrastructure) to explain where discussion, technical work, and governance happen. Keep the explanation relevant to the human's goal.
3. If suggesting active work, check original [issues](https://github.com/1Hive/1hive.org/issues), [pull requests](https://github.com/1Hive/1hive.org/pulls), or [Forum discussions](https://forum.1hive.org). Check for an existing owner or duplicate effort. Record the date and links. If inaccessible, suggest an evergreen documentation task and label current availability unverified.
4. Recommend one small contribution and define what a completed result would look like. Say why it fits, where it belongs, and what needs confirmation before starting.

## Return

- A short explanation of 1Hive and the parts relevant to this human.
- One recommended next step with scope, a concrete deliverable, and source links.
- Any access gaps, uncertain facts, or coordination needed.

## Boundaries

Do not register accounts, join private spaces, post introductions, claim tasks, or commit the human to work without authorization. Do not treat wiki descriptions as proof that a service, opportunity, or reward is currently available. Preserve the difference between the community's aspirations and verified current capabilities.
