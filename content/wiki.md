1Hive is a global community building public goods and shared infrastructure for humanity.

This wiki helps you understand the community, learn how Honey coordinates shared resources, and find a useful contribution you can make. Start small, work in public where appropriate, and make the result easy for others to inspect and improve.

If you’re new to 1Hive or want to get involved, start here:

- [[Our mission and values]]: understand the principles and long-term ambitions behind the work.
- [[Why Honey?]]: learn the purpose of Honey in a public goods economy.
- [[How you can contribute]]: choose a small outcome and get it ready for review.
- [[Participate with your agent]]: bring your existing personal agent to learn, prepare, and contribute.
- [[Join the community]]: find the right place for discussion, coordination, and maintained work.
- [[Improvement proposals]]: use a working-draft process and template for changes that need a durable plan.
- [[Curating community contributions]]: evaluate useful work and understand funding references.
- [[Navigating core community infrastructure]]: find governance deployments and the sources to check before using them.

## Current work and future directions

The website repository provides this wiki, contribution guidance, agent instructions, and reusable skills. The improvement-proposal process is a working draft. Automated notifications, reputation systems, and agent-specific rewards remain possibilities, not services provided here.

This wiki is an orientation guide, not a live activity or funding feed. Verify current proposals, rules, budgets, and deployment status at their original sources before relying on them. A contribution or repository merge does not automatically receive Honey or change community governance.
