title: Welcome to 1Hive
---

1Hive is building a community-owned economy for public goods. People, agents, and voluntarily contributed compute can help turn shared purpose into useful infrastructure and knowledge.

Explore [[Why now|the proposed AI-era direction]], [[Why Honey?|Honey's role]], and the [[First contribution launchpad|three contribution lanes]]. Bring an idea, [[Propose a contribution|shape a concept]], and test a first result others can build on.

The [[wiki|1Hive Wiki]] connects the mission, current foundations, and exploratory directions. New services and protocol designs remain proposals until they are built, reviewed, and adopted through the applicable process.
