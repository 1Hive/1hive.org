---
title: Welcome to 1Hive
---

1Hive is a global community building a community-owned economy for public goods. Honey, open infrastructure, and useful contributions are at the center of that effort.

Visit the [[wiki|1Hive Wiki]] to learn about Honey, community governance, contribution pathways, and the tools that support shared public goods work.

Start with one useful contribution, directly or with the [[Participate with your agent|personal agent you already use]]. Explore [[How you can contribute]], or use the [[Improvement proposals|working-draft improvement process]] to prepare a larger change.
